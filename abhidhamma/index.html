Abhidhamma study

Mind, matter and the conditions that join them

Two companions to Bhikkhu Bodhi's A Comprehensive Manual of Abhidhamma, made for slow, deep study.

Abhidhamma Atlas

Fifteen plates on a seven-level path, with 3D models, stories from the Buddha's life and the Jātakas, a clickable concept map, and tools for slow study. Read it on screen or print it.

The atlas as a PDF

Sixty-seven A4 pages, one sheet per page, for reading offline or printing.

Nāma–rūpa model

An interactive model of mind and matter: the relations between them, a lifespan of moments, the kalāpas, and the factors around one citta.
Made as a study aid, not as an authority: every plate names its sources, and the readings of the stories are marked as study aids. To suggest a correction or add a plate, see the source and its CONTRIBUTING guide.
