question: In dreamless sleep the life-continuum flows unbroken. Does that make it the self that lasts?
answer: >-
  No. It is a series of cittas, each arising and ceasing, all of one kind because one kamma produced them.
  What continues is a stream, as a river continues; no thing stays the same in it. It is also a result: nobody
  chooses it.
---
<i>Bhavaṅga</i>, the factor of becoming, is the resultant citta that keeps a life continuous whenever no active process is running, as in dreamless sleep. It is the same kind of citta as rebirth-linking (<i>paṭisandhi</i>) and death (<i>cuti</i>), and it takes the same object, the one presented at the end of the previous life. It is not a soul or a hidden self: it arises and dissolves like any other citta. It shows how continuity is possible without an enduring subject.
