question: >-
  The thirty-one planes are worlds, and each also matches kinds of citta. Is a plane a place or a state of
  mind?
answer: >-
  In the Abhidhamma it is both. Rebirth in a plane is the result of the matching kind of citta, and in this
  life the same kinds of citta come and go. The cosmology maps mind onto world.
---
The cosmos is laid out as thirty-one planes of existence, matched to levels of mind: four woeful planes, seven happy sense-sphere planes, sixteen fine-material and four immaterial Brahmā worlds. Each is reached by the kamma or the jhāna that corresponds to it, so the cosmology is psychology projected along the axis of rebirth. Every plane belongs to saṃsāra and is impermanent; even the longest lifespan ends.
