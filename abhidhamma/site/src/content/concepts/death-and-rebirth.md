question: Nothing passes from the dying moment to rebirth-linking. What connects the two lives?
answer: >-
  Condition alone. The last process's kamma and its object condition the rebirth-linking citta, as a seal
  leaves its mark in wax or an echo answers a voice: Visuddhimagga XVII's similes. The new citta is neither
  the old one nor unrelated to it.
---
Death (<i>cuti</i>) is the cutting off of the life faculty in one existence. Rebirth-linking (<i>paṭisandhi</i>), the first citta of the next, follows without interval, conditioned by the kamma that ripens at death and taking the object shown to the last javanas: a kamma, a sign of kamma or a sign of destiny. Nothing passes across. The commentaries compare it to a seal and its impression, or an echo and its sound: the new being is “neither the same nor another” (<i>na ca so na ca añño</i>).
