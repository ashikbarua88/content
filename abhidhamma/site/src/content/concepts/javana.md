question: >-
  Seeing is the result of past kamma, but the javanas make new kamma. Where is the room for choice in a moment
  of seeing?
answer: >-
  Not in the seeing, nor in adverting, which only turns. The room is in the javanas, shaped by how attention
  turned at determining and by habit. Wise attention (yoniso manasikāra) is what makes the same mango an
  occasion for clarity instead of greed.

<i>Javana</i>, impulsion, is the active phase of a process: usually seven like cittas in which the moment's ethical quality is fixed. Only here is kamma made, by worldlings and trainees; in an arahant the javanas are merely functional (<i>kiriya</i>) and leave no result. It is here that wise or unwise attention (<i>yoniso / ayoniso manasikāra</i>) bears fruit, so the javana is the point of practical freedom within an otherwise conditioned process.
