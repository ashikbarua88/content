question: >-
  Dependent origination is usually read across three lives. Where, in a single moment, could you watch one
  link become the next?
answer: >-
  From feeling to craving, in the javanas that follow a pleasant feeling. The Vibhaṅga also teaches the whole
  series within a single moment of citta, in its Abhidhamma section, so the chain is not only a story of
  lives.

“When this is, that is; from the arising of this, that arises.” The twelve links show how ignorance and craving keep the round (<i>vaṭṭa</i>) of existence turning across three lives: past causes, present results, present causes, future results. Saṃsāra appears as a process with no self that transmigrates, which avoids both eternalism (<i>sassata-diṭṭhi</i>) and annihilationism (<i>uccheda-diṭṭhi</i>). Read in reverse order, the same formula describes cessation.
