question: Insight looks at the same dhammas the Abhidhamma lists. What changes when a list becomes something seen?
answer: >-
  Impermanence, suffering and not-self are seen in present dhammas, not inferred about them. Knowing their
  rise and fall replaces knowing about it. The list stays useful as a map of where to look.

Insight (<i>vipassanā</i>) is wisdom (<i>paññā</i>) that sees mind and matter as they are: marked by impermanence (<i>anicca</i>), suffering (<i>dukkha</i>) and non-self (<i>anattā</i>). It proceeds through the seven purifications and the ten insight knowledges, from defining mind and matter to equanimity about formations. Insight takes conditioned dhammas as its object: it ripens the mind for the path, but it does not itself realise Nibbāna.
