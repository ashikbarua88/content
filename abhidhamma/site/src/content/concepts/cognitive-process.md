question: If seeing is over before you know you are seeing, what is the "I see" that follows?
answer: >-
  Later processes at the mind door take the earlier one as their object and add concepts: colour, then shape,
  then "mango", then "I". The "I see" is built afterwards by those processes, out of material the seeing
  itself did not contain.
---
Cognition runs in fixed series, the order of mind (<i>citta-niyāma</i>). An object strikes a door, the life-continuum vibrates and is cut off, and adverting, sense consciousness, receiving, investigating, determining, javana and registration follow, each conditioning the next by proximity (<i>anantara</i>). Nobody directs the sequence; it is a lawful succession of impersonal events. Knowing a whole thing takes many such processes, five-door and mind-door in turn.
