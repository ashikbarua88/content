question: Matter knows nothing. Why does a teaching about liberation list 28 kinds of it?
answer: >-
  Because experience is partly made of it. The sense bases and their objects are rūpa, and the body is the
  first ground of mindfulness. Seeing matter arise and break up loosens the hold of "this body is mine".

Matter is so called because it is “afflicted” (<i>ruppati</i>): it is altered by cold, heat, hunger and thirst. Its core is the four great elements (<i>mahābhūta</i>): earth as hardness, water as cohesion, fire as heat, air as motion and support; twenty-four kinds are derived from them (<i>upādā-rūpa</i>). Matter knows no object (<i>anārammaṇa</i>). It arises in inseparable groups (<i>kalāpa</i>) of eight or more, from four origins (kamma, citta, temperature, nutriment), and a unit of it lasts seventeen mind-moments.
