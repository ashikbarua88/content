question: If a person is only a convention, who is responsible for a deed?
answer: >-
  The continuum is. The cittas whose volition was kamma condition later cittas in the same stream, and
  convention names that stream "Devadatta". The analysis takes away only the idea of an owner standing outside
  the process. The Milindapañha's answer to whether the one reborn is the same person is "neither the same nor
  another".

The Abhidhamma speaks in two registers. Conventional truth (<i>sammuti-sacca</i>) names persons, chariots and selves; it is valid for living and speaking, and it is not a lie. Ultimate truth (<i>paramattha-sacca</i>) speaks of <i>dhammas</i>: phenomena that bear their own nature (<i>sabhāva</i>) and cannot be analysed further. Names and the things they designate are concepts (<i>paññatti</i>) and belong to the first register; the four ultimate realities belong to the second. Moving between them does not deny the world. It redescribes it so that the view of a lasting self (<i>sakkāya-diṭṭhi</i>) finds no ground.
