question: A path lasts a single moment, yet it changes a person for good. How can one moment do so much?
answer: >-
  The path citta cuts defilements that would otherwise go on conditioning later cittas. Its effect is what
  never arises again. Fruition follows at once, taking the same Nibbāna as its object.
---
Path and fruit (<i>magga, phala</i>) are the supramundane (<i>lokuttara</i>) cittas that take Nibbāna as object. Each of the four paths arises only once and abandons defilements for good (<i>samuccheda-pahāna</i>): stream-entry, once-return, non-return, arahantship. Fruition follows at once, on the same object. Here the eightfold path is complete in a single moment, its factors working together. With the fourth path the round of kamma and rebirth is cut at its roots, ignorance and craving.
